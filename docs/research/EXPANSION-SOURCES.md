# Assessment catalog expansion evidence

Checked: **October 2, 2026**. Scope: five assessment records added to the local v2 catalog. This is a public-source audit, not full manual verification or independent clinical validation. No private class packet, copyrighted test items, scoring tables, or patient data were copied.

The machine-readable data are in `../site/expanded-assessments.json`. Each claim has source IDs, an exact locator, method, relevant population/sample/cutoff where available, and a status. `source-checked` means the source supports the claim; it does not imply that the test is equally valid for every person or setting. `not-reported` means the statistic was unavailable in the checked sources, not that the test has no evidence.

## TNL-2

- [PRO-ED current product page](https://proedinc.com/products-14560.html): Description gives **5:0–15:11**; Improvements on the same page gives **4–15**. Time **15–20 minutes**, individual administration, Level B, authors Gillam/Pearson, ©2017, materials, and narrative focus verified.
- [PRO-ED 2025–2026 assessment catalog, p. 64](https://publications.proedsoftware.com/view/1000385236/64/): explicitly gives **4:0–15:11**. The finder includes age 4 (48 months) but flags the contradiction. Check the applicable manual before clinical administration to a 4-year-old.
- [Magimairaj et al. (2022), accessible author manuscript](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED620387.pdf), journal pp. 406, 410–411 and Table 3; [article DOI](https://doi.org/10.1044/2021_LSHSS-21-00129): reports normative-sample alpha **.81 comprehension / .87 production** from the manual. The research cohort's item agreement ranges **90.65%–93.84%** across site/occasion. This is percent agreement, not Pearson r or ICC. Cohort 3 had 154 participants initially; a random 20% per occasion was rescored, but the exact rescored n is not stated.

No sensitivity/specificity pair, cutoff, retest coefficient, total reliability coefficient, or primary-source normative n was verified. The publisher reports U.S. standardization during 2013–2015. A review table's n1,310 was located but was not imported as directly verified publisher data. The 2022 repeated measurements took place around an intervention and do not establish ordinary untreated retest reliability.

## OASES

- [Developer/publisher overview](https://stutteringtherapyresources.com/pages/oases): **S ages 7–12, 60 items; T 13–17, 80 items; A 18+, 100 items**. Approximately **15–20 minutes** to complete plus **5 minutes** scoring. The catalog has one record with three forms.
- [Publisher response-form/manual requirements](https://stutteringtherapyresources.com/products/oases-spanish): the age-appropriate forms require the Technical/Scoring Manual. Translation availability does not establish shared norms or universal language equivalence.
- [Yaruss & Quesal (2006), original primary development paper](https://www.csun.edu/~ainslab/readings/PastLabMembers/Vince/Yaruss_OASES.pdf); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfludis.2006.02.002): journal pp. 100–101 report adult development-section alpha **.92–.97**, based on the near-final CASES form (173 adults ages 18–70) before minor wording changes. Section 5.3, pp. 103–104, gives adult retest **r .90–.97**, **n14**, ages 22–65, **10–14 days**, no intervening therapy.

Those 2006 statistics are explicitly historical adult-form evidence. They are not labeled current S/T coefficients. Current developer scoring is on a 1–5 scale; the historical paper used a 20–100 scale. Historical score cutoffs were excluded. No stuttering-presence sensitivity/specificity pair or current-form total reliability estimate was verified. The impact measure should contribute the speaker's perspective to a broader evaluation.

## WAB-R

- [Pearson US overview](https://www.pearsonassessments.com/en-us/Store/Professional-Assessments/Speech-%26-Language/Western-Aphasia-Battery-Revised/p/100000194): ages **18–89**, release **2006**, Level B, task scope, materials, and administration paths verified. The publisher describes **30–45 minutes** plus **45–60 additional minutes** for reading, writing, praxis, and construction; bedside **15 minutes**. Numeric finder duration uses the first part, and the display names the supplemental time. A complete Part 1 plus Part 2 estimate of 75–105 minutes is a derived sum, not a separate publisher claim.
- [Pearson UK psychometric information](https://www.pearsonclinical.co.uk/en-gb/Store/Professional-Assessments/Speech-%26-Language/Western-Aphasia-Battery-Revised/p/P100009022): original WAB **150 aphasia / 59 controls**, second standardization **215 / 63**, and revised/adapted tasks **n20 (14 aphasia, 6 controls)**. These sets stay separate. They are not summed into one WAB-R norm n.
- [Senthilkumar et al. (2026), primary accuracy study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13151572/); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1044/2026_AJSLP-25-00361): published March 25, 2026. At **AQ 93.7/100**, sensitivity **77%**, specificity **64%**, accuracy **75%**, in **171 English-speaking acute ischemic stroke patients**, assessed within **2 weeks**, compared with discourse classification from a picture description. These values describe that reference definition and patient spectrum. The proposed AQ96.7 threshold was not installed as a new official threshold.
- [Rao et al. (2022), PPA administration-mode study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9150668/), Participants and Table 3: **Lin concordance .942 for AQ**, **19 people / 23 paired data sets**, in-person versus telehealth **6–90 days** apart. Displayed under mode agreement, not internal consistency or a universal total reliability value.

No primary current-edition same-mode retest, alpha, or interrater coefficient was verified. Original-WAB results and student presentations were excluded as substitutes. An AQ in the accepted normal range can miss mild discourse impairment in the 2026 study. Bedside and full-battery information also differ.

## CLQT+

- [Pearson US overview](https://www.pearsonassessments.com/en-us/Store/Professional-Assessments/Cognition-%26-Neuro/Cognitive-Linguistic-Quick-Test-Plus/p/100000459): ages **18:0–89:11**, **15–30 minutes**, Level B, CLQT2001/CLQT+2017, paper-and-pencil, Traditional and Aphasia paths. Spanish applies to **Traditional Administration only**.
- [Pearson brochure](https://www.pearsonassessments.com/content/dam/school/global/clinical/us/assets/clqt/clqt-plus-brochure.pdf), PDF p. 3: original development pilot/studies **n13, n92, n154, n119**; CLQT+ aphasia-path clinical sample **n76** with left-hemisphere stroke. Criterion cut scores and severity ratings combine those data with clinical expertise. Two age groups are 18–69 and 70–89.
- [Pearson task interpretation guidance](https://www.pearsonclinical.ca/content/dam/school/global/clinical/canada/programs/CLQT/CLQT-Interpretive-Information-Flyer.pdf): language impairment can influence Traditional-path memory interpretation; task scores require consideration of overlapping demands.

Public official sources checked here give sample descriptions but no exact reliability coefficients or matched diagnostic-accuracy pair. A Pearson-authored 2017 slide deck was found through a third-party mirror with a split-half .91 figure for an Auditory Comprehension task, but its complete original-host document was not obtained; the figure was excluded. Secondary reports of original-CLQT retest/rater values were also excluded. The finder describes this as a focused overview, not proof that cognition is comprehensively characterized by one brief test.

## CADL-3

- [PRO-ED current product page](https://proedinc.com/products-14650.html): ages **18–95+**, **30 minutes**, individual manual scoring, Level B, ©2018, authors, 50 items, materials and functional areas.
- [PRO-ED 2025–2026 speech-language catalog, p. 64](https://publications.proedsoftware.com/view/836271174/64/): confirms edition/product context.

The product's Technical and Statistical Characteristics gives **alpha .94** and **test–retest .94**. Its norm sample is **115 adults with neurogenic communication disorders**, collected **2014–2016**. The page does **not** explicitly identify the alpha-analysis n, retest n, retest interval, or exact retest coefficient method. Those remain null/unknown; n115 is used only for the standardization record. No rater coefficient or numerical sensitivity/specificity pair was available. Norms are a clinical sample, not healthy general-population norms. Spanish/Catalan adaptation statistics were not transferred to the English record.

## Data checks

Every referenced source ID resolves within its assessment. All five age/time filters use months/minutes consistently; OASES and CADL-3 use null for their open upper age bounds. Missing psychometric numbers are never stored as zero. Only publisher/developer material and original research results appear as affirmative evidence. Clinical cautions are labeled as interpretation where they extend beyond literal publisher claims.
