## CASL-2

- [Current WPS publisher page](https://www.wpspublish.com/casl-2-comprehensive-assessment-of-spoken-language-second-edition.html), specifications and Technical Information: Second Edition, 2017, ages 3–21, 14 spoken-language tests, age-norm sample **2,394**, WPS **Level C**. The publisher currently lists the requested edition.
- [Official Pearson distribution page](https://www.pearsonassessments.com/en-us/Store/Professional-Assessments/Developmental-Early-Childhood/Comprehensive-Assessment-of-Spoken-Language-%7C-Second-Edition/p/100001922), Overview and kit listing: 5–10 minutes per test, approximately 45 minutes for GLAI, supplier qualification **B**. The qualification discrepancy is attributed by supplier rather than resolved by silently picking one.
- [WPS oral-language webinar handout](https://pages.wpspublish.com/hubfs/Webinar%20PPT%20Slides/Oral%20Language%20PPT%20handout%20073024.pdf), PDF p. 13 / slide 50: GLAI estimate 45–60 minutes. PDF p. 17 / slides 65–67 describes U.S. norm representation and sufficient English proficiency. The draft retains the different timing estimates and does not call the individual-test estimate a full battery time.
- [Ferrell and Rogoff publisher-hosted original equivalency study](https://www.wpspublish.com/media/content-wps/CASL-2_equivalency.pdf), pp. 2–4 and Table 2, p. 6: **study-wide n27**, ages 3–19, average retest interval two weeks (10–21 days), counterbalanced remote/in-person modes with different examiners. Uncorrected standard-score correlations: Expressive Vocabulary .98; Sentence Expression .93; Grammatical Morphemes .85; Grammaticality Judgment .99; Inference .99; Pragmatic Language 1.00. Raw-score ICCs .98–1.00 are likewise tied to this design. Each person received three tests, so n27 is not a per-subtest sample count. These are not silently substituted for same-mode manual retest or independent rescoring reliability.
- [Ferrell / WPS sensitivity and specificity technical paper](https://www.wpspublish.com/media/wysiwyg/casl-2_sensitivity_specificity_1.pdf), p. 5, Table 2: **GLAI cutoff 85, sensitivity .74, specificity .84**, mixed clinical diagnoses, ages 3–21, clinical n271. The comparator n is not printed beside this table. Table 4, p. 7: **cutoff 80, sensitivity .89, specificity .98**, selected moderate-to-severe subgroup excluding learning disability and developmental delay, clinical n141 versus controls n2,194. The record shows both contexts separately; selected-subgroup accuracy is not the assessment's universal accuracy.
- Current WPS product page, IMPORTANT NOTE: manuals purchased before **February 15, 2017** require the updated Grammaticality Judgment norms supplement; WPS says Hub scoring already incorporates the correction.

Gaps: score/age/method-specific internal consistency and GLAI reliability were not verified from full manual tables. Ages 3–21 are the public specification; month filter 36–263 includes that full stated age band while the record directs users to test-specific norms. The older Pearson page titled “CASL Core and Supplementary Tests by Age Band” contains original-CASL tasks and was excluded as CASL-2 evidence.

## OWLS-II

- [WPS Quick Reference Guide](https://www.wpspublish.com/media/wpsmedia/owls-ii_quick_reference.pdf), p. 1 scale chart: **Form A LC/OE 3:0–21:11; RC/WE 5:0–21:11**. LC 10–20 minutes; OE 10–30; RC 10–30; WE 15–30. Page 2: **all Form B scales 5:0–21:11**. Scales may be administered separately or together. The filter uses broad age coverage 36–263 months, with narrower written/Form B ages prominently stated. Numeric times describe an individual scale, not a four-scale battery.
- [Current WPS publisher page](https://www.wpspublish.com/owls-ii-oral-and-written-language-scales-second-edition.html), specifications and norm summary: Second Edition, 2011, standardization n2,123 from 31 states, U.S. demographic representation, **WPS Level C**. [Pearson U.S. distribution page](https://www.pearsonassessments.com/en-us/Store/Professional-Assessments/Speech-%26-Language/OWLS-II-Oral-and-Written-Language-Scales-%7C-Second-Edition/p/100000293) lists **B**, which is separately attributed.
- [WPS webinar](https://pages.wpspublish.com/hubfs/Webinar%20PPT%20Slides/Oral%20Language%20PPT%20handout%20073024.pdf), PDF p. 12 / slide 47: standardization n2,123; separate validation samples n241 with speech/language disorders and n114 with psychological/behavioral intervention needs. Those are sample descriptions, not sensitivity/specificity coefficients.
- [Current WPS data-collection page](https://pages.wpspublish.com/data-collection), Current Data Collection Opportunities: WPS is recruiting for OWLS-3. That is evidence of development activity; no released OWLS-3 product or its norms were established. OWLS-II remains the requested, currently listed record.

Gaps: public checked primary materials did not verify numerical internal consistency, test–retest, interrater, Overall Language reliability, or matched sensitivity/specificity with cutoff. Alternate-form availability does not itself quantify reliability. Normative language-proficiency criteria require manual verification.

## REEL-4

- [PRO-ED complete-kit page](https://proedinc.com/products-14880.html), More Information and Description: Fourth Edition, ©2020; Virginia L. Brown, Kenneth R. Bzoch, Richard League; **birth through 36 months**, **20 minutes**, individual **caregiver interview**, **Level B**. Language Ability comprises receptive and expressive subtests; Vocabulary Inventory is supplementary. Current publisher listings retain REEL-4.
- Same page, normative paragraph: **n1,019** U.S. infants/toddlers; demographics aligned with the 2019 child population; stratification by gender, race, Hispanic status, and region.
- [PRO-ED virtual manual listing](https://proedinc.com/products-14881e.html), ©2020 and author/qualification specifications corroborate the edition.

Gaps: the product page states that average reliability exceeds .90 without specifying calculation, score, subgroup, or table. This is retained as an attributed general statement, not relabeled as Cronbach alpha or a Language Ability coefficient. Retest stability and diagnostic studies are described, but public paired sensitivity/specificity, thresholds, study n, retest interval, and score-specific coefficients remain unverified. A small repository-hosted original application study was inspected but excluded as normative-coefficient authority: it applied age equivalents beyond the publisher age range and repeated coefficients without adequate edition/subtest/sample context.

## Bayley-III

- [Pearson archived Third Edition page](https://www.pearsonassessments.com/en-us/Store/Professional-Assessments/Developmental-Early-Childhood/Bayley-4/p/100000123), Overview and notice: **Third Edition, ages 1–42 months, Level B, 30–90 minutes depending on age; no longer available for purchase**. The path contains “Bayley-4,” but the page title and product ID 100000123 explicitly identify **III**. The record retains III rather than using Fourth Edition statistics.
- [Pearson III domain guide](https://www.pearsonassessments.com/content/dam/school/global/clinical/us/assets/bayley-iii/bayley-iii-domains.pdf), pp. 1–2: five developmental domains; Language contains Receptive and Expressive Communication. This is a broad developmental assessment with a language component.
- [Pearson Technical Report 2](https://www.pearsonassessments.com/content/dam/school/global/clinical/us/assets/bayley-iii/bayley-iii-technical-report-2.pdf), pp. 1–2: norms represent the **2000 U.S. population**, **9.8% clinical cases**. [Official III-to-4 comparison](https://www.pearsonassessments.com/content/dam/school/global/clinical/us/assets/bayley-4/bayley-3-to-4-comparison.pdf), III column / Norms and Clinical Studies: **III data collected 2001–2002**. Collection dates and census target are different facts.
- Pearson III page, Language sufficiency FAQ: further assessment is needed to pinpoint intervention targets. Scoring FAQ: no cross-domain overall composite. The 30–90-minute estimate is identified as the developmental battery; a language-only duration was not verified.
- [Technical Report 1](https://www.pearsonassessments.com/content/dam/school/global/clinical/us/assets/bayley-iii/bayley-iii-technical-report-1.pdf), pp. 1–3: standard age range and cautions for descriptive use in older individuals with severe delays. That guidance does not extend normative ages.
- [Krogh and Væver original 2016 study](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/sjop.12333), public Abstract: **Danish n43**, ages 2 and 3; Language scores differed from U.S. norms and showed significant associations with parent CDI. This is retained as a cultural/validity caution, not a diagnostic accuracy pair or a U.S. reliability coefficient.

Gaps: inspected primary technical text did not provide total normative n or language-specific internal, retest, rater, or Language Composite reliability coefficients. Commonly repeated n1,700 was not entered without an inspected primary count. General clinical-discrimination claims and r=.83 Language/WPPSI-III VIQ convergent correlation are not sensitivity/specificity. The current product lists publication 2005, while III technical reports cite Bayley (2006); both dates are attributed. Normative scores above the stated age range, arbitrary translation equivalence, and a language-only test duration remain unsupported.
