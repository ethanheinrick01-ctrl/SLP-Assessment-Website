## Numerical study

The original [Petersen, Tonn, Spencer, and Foster paper](https://pubs.asha.org/doi/10.1044/2019_LSHSS-18-0129) was reopened through Exa. Its Abstract and Method / Participants confirm **31 children: 10 with language disorder, 21 with typical language; ages 5:9–9:7**, recruited from three elementary schools. Procedure / Assessment Overview specifies an **English-only** dynamic task given to the bilingual Spanish/English participants.

**Table 3, combined dynamic-assessment variables row: sensitivity 90%, specificity 90.5%.** These are the linear discriminant function results. Footnote a says no interpretable single cutoff can be derived for the combined variables. The website correctly ties the pair to that model and does not use it as PPVT-5 accuracy. The higher abstract ranges also include logistic-regression results and should not replace this specific Table 3 pair.

No numerical correction is needed. Recommended wording clarifies the task's English-only administration and the authors' call for replication and cross-validation in Limitations and Future Directions. The study's explicit Information / History gives first online publication **December 17, 2019**, and issue publication **January 8, 2020**; the existing citation year 2020 is appropriate. Crawl dates were not treated as publication dates.
