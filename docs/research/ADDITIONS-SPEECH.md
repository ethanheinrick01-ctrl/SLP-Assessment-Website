# Arizona-4 and KSPT additions

Checked October 3, 2026. Arizona-4 was explicitly selected by the user. Protected test items and normative conversion tables are not reproduced.

[WPS kit specifications](https://www.wpspublish.com/arizona-4-print-kit) establish the fourth revision, 2017 publication, and Level C requirement. The [official PRO-ED distribution page](https://proedinc.com/products-14723.html), Technical Information, supplies the standardized range 1:6–21:11 and normative n3,192. Its clinical sample n50 comprises individuals with diagnosed speech sound disorders receiving treatment; this is a separate group, not a complete diagnostic-study denominator. No numerical reliability coefficients or matched cutoff-specific sensitivity/specificity are provided there.

[WPS administration slides](https://pages.wpspublish.com/hubfs/Online%20Assessment%20Tools%20Every%20Practitioner%20%20Needs%20in%20Telepractice.pdf), PDF p. 33, specify 5–20 minutes **each** for the articulation tests. Word and Sentence Articulation may be administered separately or together. Phonology is coded from Word Articulation productions. The finder therefore uses a quoted articulation-task duration, not a verified whole-battery duration.

[Northern Speech Services' developer-endorsed KSPT listing](https://www.northernspeech.com/assessments/kaufman-speech-praxis-test-kspt/) confirms 2:0–5:11, 5–15 minutes, and a combined Manual & Testing Guide ©2026. [Wayne State University Press](https://wsupress.wayne.edu/9780814324684/) dates the original test booklets to July 1, 1995. The current developer listing does not establish new norm collection. Retailers calling the product KSPT-Revised do not justify borrowing or inventing updated norms. The profile distinguishes the current manual format from the original test.

Public KSPT sources state that the manual includes normative and reliability information without publishing numerical coefficients or normative group counts. Those fields remain explicitly unverified. [ASHA's current Childhood Apraxia of Speech portal](https://www.asha.org/practice-portal/clinical-topics/childhood-apraxia-of-speech/), Assessment and Diagnosing CAS, supports comprehensive motor speech assessment, dynamic assessment, and expert clinical judgment. This guidance is identified as clinical context rather than a KSPT validation study.
