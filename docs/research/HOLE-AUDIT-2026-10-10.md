# Hole audit · 10 October 2026

Purpose: close every figure that still rendered as "—" on the spec sheet where a public source exists, and say plainly where none does. Every figure below has an evidence record with its source, method, sample and status. Nothing here is an LSU-verified value; faculty validation is pending for the whole site.

Status words used in records: **source-checked** (confirmed what the source reports), **conflicting** (sources disagree; both kept), **inherited** (carried forward from an earlier edition or a secondary summary; not verified for the current edition), **not-reported** (no public figure found).
