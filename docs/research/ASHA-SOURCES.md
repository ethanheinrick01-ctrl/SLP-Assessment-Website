## Interpretation boundaries

- ASHA has not endorsed this site, its catalog, rankings, or saved shortlist.
- Portal guidance, Evidence Maps, research studies, and publisher test specifications are different evidence types. ASHA links do not validate a catalog entry's psychometric numbers.
- Page scope is retained: idiopathic childhood speech sound disorders, developmental spoken language, acquired adult aphasia, lifespan executive function, adult TBI, and lifespan AAC.
- Language, dialect, disability access, and testing modifications can affect interpretation. A filter match does not establish that a test is appropriate for a particular client.
- No copyrighted test items, score tables, Portal illustrations, or study figures were copied. Guidance is concise paraphrase with public links.
- Orellana et al. (2019) was considered but not added; its publisher article returned inconsistent access. No estimates from that review were copied into the site.
