## C. Not recommended with current public evidence

- **VMPAC** (Hayden & Square, 1999): out of print since January 2016 per Buros; the "VMPAC-R" seen in an uploaded manual chapter could not be verified with a publisher.
- **Verbal Dyspraxia Profile** (Jelm, 2001): McCauley & Strand (2008) found no adequate reliability, validity or norm documentation.
- **ALPHA-R** (Lowe, 2000): one review lists n1,310 from four states and ages 3:0–8:11, but no coefficients are public and current availability is unclear; the original ALPHA is a free download.
- **Articulation Test Center / Hive** (Little Bee Speech app): criterion-referenced; vendor states no plans to standardize; no reliability or validity studies.
- **Articulation Assessment Toolkit** (Smarty Ears app): no psychometric documentation.
- **KLPA-2**: superseded by KLPA-3, which is already on the site.
