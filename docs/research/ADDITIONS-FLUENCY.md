# Addition: SSI-4 (October 10, 2026)

Added the Stuttering Severity Instrument, Fourth Edition (Riley, 2009; PRO-ED) as the second fluency instrument beside the OASES, so the fluency family has a severity measure and an impact measure.

## Limits

- The PRO-ED product page did not render for direct reading on the check date; its ages and testing time are taken from the indexed listing text and marked as such. The lower age bound is recorded as a publisher conflict (2:0 vs 2:10) and the numeric filter uses 24 months.
- No internal consistency, test–retest, or diagnostic-accuracy figure was found in public sources. The examiner's manual was not read. The instrument grades severity; it is not a diagnostic classifier, so no sensitivity/specificity pair applies.
- The 2025 study concerns a translated research version and is included as judge-reliability context, not as evidence for the English norms.
